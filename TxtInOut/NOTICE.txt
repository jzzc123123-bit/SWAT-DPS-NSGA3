Minimal example. No research-basin inputs or observations are included.
Areas, soil settings, management and daily weather were specified for this example.
Weather coverage: 2000-2050 inclusive; 18,628 daily records per weather file.
Warmup: 2000-2004; evaluation and annual RBF feedback: 2005-2050.
Weather uses seed 20261026, variable rain events and wet/dry year clusters.
This wet-climate example spans approximately 2175-4188 mm annual rainfall.
Weather-generator monthly statistics summarize the same illustrative daily inputs.
Temperature has daily fluctuations and an illustrative 0.02 deg C/year trend.
These illustrative series are neither observations nor GCM climate projections.
File layouts and standard database excerpts are adapted from the public SWATdata demo:
https://github.com/chrisschuerz/SWATdata
